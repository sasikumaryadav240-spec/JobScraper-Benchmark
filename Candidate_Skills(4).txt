PART E --- JOB MATCHING AND RESUME RULES
1. Match classifications
Strong match: core requirements directly supported by professional experience or relevant hands-on practice.
Good match: most core requirements supported; limited gaps.
Partial match: adjacent fundamentals exist, but important skills/tools are missing.
Weak match: only a small portion of core responsibilities is supported.
No match: essential capability is absent.

2. Alternative tools
Splunk OR Sentinel: credit the platform actually used; do not require both.
Nessus / Qualys / OpenVAS: credit Nessus practical use and note the missing platform.
EDR alternatives: credit demonstrated Defender XDR/LimaCharlie exposure while noting missing vendor-specific production experience.
Cisco / Juniper / Aruba: general networking knowledge transfers, but vendor configuration experience must not be invented.
ServiceNow / Jira Service Management / Freshservice: ITSM concepts do not equal hands-on platform administration.
Hyper-V / VMware: virtualization fundamentals do not equal production hypervisor administration.
Azure / AWS: cloud fundamentals do not equal production cloud engineering.

3. Experience eligibility
The candidate is a fresher for cybersecurity, IT support, system administration and network administration employment unless new professional employment is explicitly added.
Professional experience currently recorded:
- MERN Stack Developer Intern --- Varnarc Pvt. Ltd. --- January 2026 to April 2026.
Never count SOC labs as SOC employment, security projects as cybersecurity employment, home labs as system administration employment, Packet Tracer exercises as professional network engineering employment, coursework as professional IT support employment, or platform familiarity as production administration.
Experience filter: accept roles where the mandatory minimum experience is 0, 1 or 2 years, even if the advertised maximum is higher. Reject roles where the mandatory minimum is 3 or more years. Distinguish mandatory requirements from preferred experience and interpret ambiguous wording carefully.

4. Match scoring model
Required technical skills: 35%
Core responsibilities: 25%
Experience compatibility: 15%
Tools and technologies: 10%
Education and certifications: 5%
Transferable skills: 10%
Total: 100%. Include jobs scoring at least 50%, subject to mandatory eligibility filters. Do not inflate scores.

5. Resume tailoring
Tailor each resume to the actual role family and job description.
Do not put all 15 families on every resume.
Select relevant, defensible skills only.
Prioritize professional experience and demonstrable projects.
Clearly label labs and independent projects.
Never present learning targets as completed skills.
Never claim enterprise production administration without actual production responsibility.
Do not add tools merely because they appear in a job description.
Preserve exact certification names and education details.
Ask when a required fact is missing; do not invent it.
